2. Your data and privacy

Your transactions, accounts, budgets and settings are stored in files on your own computer, one database file per profile. The project has no account system and receives none of that data. We do not collect, sell or share your financial information.

Some features need the internet. These are the only times the app contacts another service:

  a. Fonts. When the app opens, it loads its typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Google can see that request, including your IP address, under its own privacy policy.
  b. Update check. Once when the app opens, it asks GitHub (api.github.com) whether a newer release exists. GitHub can see that request, including your IP address. If you choose to download an update, the installer is downloaded from GitHub.
  c. Live investment prices. Only if you turn this on and enter your own API key, the app sends the stock or fund symbols you track, together with your key, to the price provider you chose: Alpha Vantage, Finnhub, StockData.org or Twelve Data. Those services have their own terms and privacy policies.

The app sends no transaction, balance, account or profile data in any of these requests.
