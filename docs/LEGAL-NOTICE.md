## 2. Local Data Storage and Privacy

Vault Spend is designed as a local-first application.

Your transactions, accounts, budgets, balances, settings, and other profile information are stored in files on your own computer, generally using one database file for each profile.

Vault Spend does not operate a user account system.

Vault Spend does not automatically upload your financial database to the Vault Spend project.

Vault Spend does not sell your financial information.

### External Network Requests

In the current version of Vault Spend, the application is designed to make external network requests only for limited features described below.

### a. Fonts

If Vault Spend loads fonts from Google Fonts, your computer may contact:

- `fonts.googleapis.com`
- `fonts.gstatic.com`

Google may receive technical information associated with those requests, including your IP address and other standard network-request information.

Google's own privacy policies govern its handling of that information.

Future versions of Vault Spend may bundle fonts directly with the application, in which case these requests may no longer occur.

### b. Update Checks

When Vault Spend starts, the application may contact GitHub through `api.github.com` to determine whether a newer version is available.

GitHub may receive technical information associated with that request, including your IP address.

If you choose to download an update, the update or installer may also be downloaded from GitHub.

GitHub's own terms and privacy policies apply to its services.

### c. Live Investment Prices

Vault Spend may provide optional investment-price features.

These features operate only if you enable them and configure a supported third-party provider.

Depending on the provider you select, Vault Spend may send:

- the ticker symbols or investment symbols you request;
- your provider API key; and
- technical information needed to make the request.

Supported providers may include:

- Alpha Vantage;
- Finnhub;
- StockData.org; and
- Twelve Data.

Each provider operates independently and is governed by its own terms, privacy policies, rate limits, pricing, and data-use rules.

### Financial Data Not Sent Through These Requests

Vault Spend is designed not to send the following information to these services as part of the requests described above:

- transaction history;
- account balances;
- account names;
- budget information;
- spending categories;
- profile databases;
- passwords; or
- recovery codes.

### Information You Voluntarily Share

If you voluntarily contact the Vault Spend project or submit information through services such as GitHub, that information is separate from the local data stored by the application.

For example, you may choose to submit:

- bug reports;
- screenshots;
- diagnostic information;
- database samples;
- feature requests; or
- other information.

Review any material before submitting it.

Do not include passwords, recovery codes, API keys, account numbers, financial records, or other sensitive information unless you intentionally choose to disclose it.

Information submitted through GitHub is also subject to GitHub's own terms and privacy policies.
