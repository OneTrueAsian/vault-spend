Vault Spend — before you install
=================================

When you run this installer, Windows will likely show a blue screen
that says "Windows protected your PC."

This is expected, and nothing is wrong — it just means this installer
isn't digitally signed with a certificate Windows already recognizes.
Vault Spend is not registered with Microsoft as a publisher.

To continue:

  1. Click "More info"
  2. Click "Run anyway"

That's it — install and open the app as normal from there.

One more thing worth knowing: Vault Spend runs entirely on your own
computer. There's no account and no cloud sync, and your financial data
stays on your machine, in a single file, for as long as you have the app
installed. The app does make a few small internet requests (its fonts from
Google, a check on GitHub for a newer version, and live prices only if you turn
them on); none of them sends your financial data. The legal notice, LEGAL-NOTICE.md
in the project's repository and in the app's Help, lists them.

Vault Spend is an independent open-source project and is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with any external
financial services or wallet providers.
